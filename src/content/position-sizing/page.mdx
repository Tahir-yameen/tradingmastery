# Position Sizing

Position sizing is the process of deciding:

> How much money you risk on each trade.

It is one of the most important skills in trading.

Because even with a good strategy…

👉 Poor risk management will still lose you money.

---

## Why Position Sizing Matters

Most beginners focus on:

* Entries
* Indicators
* Strategies

But professionals focus on:

* Risk per trade
* Capital preservation
* Consistency

👉 Because survival comes first, profits come later.

---

## The Core Rule of Risk

![Risk per trade visual](/position-sizing-risk.svg)

A simple but powerful rule:

> Never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single trade.

---

## Example

If your account is:

* $1,000
* Risk = 1%

👉 You risk $10 per trade

If you lose:

* You only lose $10
* Your account survives

---

## Position Size Depends on Stop Loss

![Stop loss distance visual](/position-sizing-stop.svg)

Position sizing is NOT random.

It depends on:

* Account size
* Risk percentage
* Stop loss distance

---

### Simple Logic:

👉 Smaller stop loss = larger position size
👉 Larger stop loss = smaller position size

---

## Why Stop Loss Matters

Your stop loss defines:

* Your risk
* Your position size
* Your trade structure

Without it:

👉 Position sizing becomes impossible

---

## Risk Per Trade vs Emotional Trading

Without position sizing:

* You overtrade
* You revenge trade
* You lose discipline

With position sizing:

* You stay consistent
* You survive losing streaks
* You grow steadily

---

## The Math Behind Trading Survival

Even profitable traders lose trades.

Example:

* Win rate: 50%
* Risk-reward: 1:2

👉 You still make profit over time

Why?

Because losses are controlled.

---

## Position Sizing Formula (Simple)

![Position sizing formula visual](/position-sizing-formula.svg)

Basic idea:

> Risk Amount ÷ Stop Loss Distance = Position Size

You don’t need to overcomplicate it.

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Risking different amounts per trade
* Increasing lot size after losses
* Ignoring stop loss distance
* Trading emotionally instead of mathematically

---

## Pro Mindset

Professional traders think:

❌ “How much can I win?”

Instead:

✅ “How much can I afford to lose?”

---

## Golden Rule of Trading

> Protect your capital first, grow it second.

---

## Simple Example Strategy

If you have:

* $500 account
* 1% risk
* 20 pip stop loss

You adjust lot size so that:

👉 Loss = $5 if stop loss hits

That’s it.

---

## Why Most Traders Fail

Not because of strategy…

But because of:

* Oversizing positions
* Ignoring risk
* Emotional decisions
