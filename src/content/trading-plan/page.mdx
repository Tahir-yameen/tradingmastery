# Trading Plan

A trading plan is a set of **clear rules that guide every decision you make in the market**.

It removes emotions and replaces them with structure.

---

## What is a Trading Plan?

![Trading plan structure](/trading-plan-structure.svg)

A trading plan defines:

* When to enter a trade
* When to exit a trade
* How much to risk
* What markets to trade
* When NOT to trade

---

## Why You Need a Trading Plan

Without a plan:

* You guess entries
* You overtrade
* You react emotionally
* You lose consistency

With a plan:

👉 You trade with discipline, not emotion

---

## Core Components of a Trading Plan

![Trading plan rules checklist](/trading-plan-rules.svg)

---

### 1. Market Selection

Decide what you trade:

* Forex
* Crypto
* Stocks
* Commodities

👉 Don’t try to trade everything

---

### 2. Timeframe Selection

Define your trading style:

* Scalping → M1, M5
* Day trading → M15, H1
* Swing trading → H4, Daily

---

### 3. Entry Rules

Clearly define:

* What setup you trade
* What conditions must be met
* What confirmations you need

Example:

* Liquidity sweep
* CHOCH confirmation
* Entry at order block

---

### 4. Exit Rules

You must define:

* Take profit levels
* Stop loss placement
* Partial exits (if used)

---

### 5. Risk Management Rules

Include:

* Max risk per trade (1–2%)
* Daily loss limit
* Weekly drawdown limit

---

### 6. Trading Hours

Define when you trade:

* London session
* New York session
* Specific hours only

👉 Avoid random trading all day

---

### 7. Setup Checklist

Before every trade ask:

* Is there a valid setup?
* Is risk-reward acceptable?
* Is structure aligned?
* Is liquidity considered?

If NO → skip trade

---

## Example Simple Trading Plan

* Trade Forex only
* 1% risk per trade
* Only trade London session
* Only take trades with:

  * Liquidity sweep
  * CHOCH
  * Order block entry
* Minimum 1:2 RR

---

## Why Simplicity Wins

![Discipline beats impulse](/trading-plan-discipline.svg)

Most beginners overcomplicate:

* Too many strategies
* Too many indicators
* No consistency

👉 Simple + consistent beats complex + random

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:

* Changing strategy every week
* Trading without written rules
* Ignoring risk management
* Entering trades outside plan

---

## Pro Tip

Your trading plan is not optional.

It is:

> Your business blueprint for the market

---

## Golden Rule

> If it’s not in your plan, you don’t trade it.
