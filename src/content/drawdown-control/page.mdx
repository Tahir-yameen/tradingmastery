# Drawdown Control

Drawdown refers to:

> The reduction in your trading account after a series of losses.

It is one of the most important risk management concepts in trading.

---

## What is Drawdown?

![Drawdown curve visual](/drawdown-control-curve.svg)

If your account goes from:

* $1,000 → $900

👉 You have a 10% drawdown

---

## Why Drawdown Matters

Drawdown is not just about losing money.

It affects:

* Psychology
* Confidence
* Decision-making
* Recovery time

---

## The Hidden Danger of Drawdown

![Risk level comparison](/drawdown-control-risk.svg)

The deeper your drawdown, the harder it is to recover.

### Example:

* 10% loss → need 11% gain to recover
* 50% loss → need 100% gain to recover

👉 Losses grow faster than recovery

---

## Types of Drawdown

---

### 1. Normal Drawdown

* Small losses over time
* Part of normal trading behavior
* Manageable and expected

---

### 2. Deep Drawdown

* Large consecutive losses
* Often caused by overtrading or poor risk control
* Dangerous for account survival

---

## How Drawdown Happens

Common reasons:

* Increasing lot size after losses
* Emotional revenge trading
* Ignoring stop losses
* Overtrading without setup

---

## How Professionals Handle Drawdown

Professional traders:

* Reduce position size during losses
* Pause trading when needed
* Stick strictly to risk rules
* Accept losses as normal

---

## Drawdown Control Rules

### 1. Daily Loss Limit

Stop trading after a fixed loss (e.g., 3–5%)

---

### 2. Weekly Loss Limit

Pause trading if weekly drawdown is hit

---

### 3. Risk Reduction After Losses

Reduce risk after losing streaks

---

## Recovery Strategy

![Recovery discipline visual](/drawdown-control-recovery.svg)

If you are in drawdown:

1. Stop increasing risk
2. Trade smaller size
3. Focus only on high-quality setups
4. Avoid revenge trading

---

## Psychological Impact

Drawdown affects traders emotionally:

* Fear of losing more
* Overconfidence after recovery
* Impatience

👉 Controlling drawdown = controlling emotions

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Trying to recover losses quickly
* Doubling lot sizes after loss
* Ignoring losing streaks
* Trading emotionally during drawdown

---

## Pro Tip

The goal is not just to make profit.

It is:

> To stay in the game long enough to become profitable.

---

## Golden Rule

> Survival first, recovery second, profit third.
