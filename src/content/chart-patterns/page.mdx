# Chart Patterns

Chart patterns are repeated formations created by price movements.

They help traders understand:

* Possible continuation of trend
* Possible reversal of trend
* Market psychology behind price action

---

## Why Chart Patterns Work

Chart patterns work because:

👉 Traders behave in predictable ways

When price moves:

* Fear and greed repeat
* Buyers and sellers react similarly at key levels
* Liquidity builds up in similar structures

So patterns repeat over time.

---

## Types of Chart Patterns

There are two main categories:

---

## 1. Reversal Patterns

These patterns suggest that the current trend may change direction.

---

### 🔻 Head and Shoulders

A bearish reversal pattern.

![Head and shoulders pattern](/chart-pattern-head-shoulders.svg)

*This setup often appears when buyers are losing control and a reversal is forming.*

Structure:

* Left shoulder
* Head (highest point)
* Right shoulder

👉 Indicates uptrend weakening

**Neckline break = sell signal**

---

### 🔺 Inverse Head and Shoulders

A bullish reversal pattern.

Structure:

* Left shoulder
* Head (lowest point)
* Right shoulder

👉 Indicates downtrend weakening

**Neckline break = buy signal**

---

### 🔻 Double Top

* Price hits resistance twice
* Fails to break higher
* Then drops

👉 Indicates bearish reversal

---

### 🔺 Double Bottom

* Price hits support twice
* Fails to break lower
* Then rises

👉 Indicates bullish reversal

---

## 2. Continuation Patterns

These patterns suggest the trend will continue after a pause.

---

### 📈 Flags (Bull & Bear Flags)

Strong trending move followed by consolidation.

* Bull flag → continuation upward
* Bear flag → continuation downward

👉 Indicates temporary pause before continuation

---

### 🔺 Triangles

There are 3 types:

![Triangle consolidation pattern](/chart-pattern-triangle.svg)

*Triangles show price compressing before a potential breakout or breakdown.*

#### Ascending Triangle

* Flat resistance
* Rising support
  👉 Bullish breakout likely

#### Descending Triangle

* Flat support
* Falling resistance
  👉 Bearish breakdown likely

#### Symmetrical Triangle

* Price compressing
  👉 Breakout direction unknown (needs confirmation)

---

## Key Concept: Market Psychology

Every pattern represents a battle:

* Buyers trying to push price up
* Sellers trying to push price down

Patterns form when:
👉 One side slowly loses strength

---

## Breakout Confirmation

Never enter blindly on pattern formation.

Wait for:

![Breakout confirmation visual](/chart-pattern-breakout.svg)

*Strong candles and clean breakout action help confirm whether the pattern is valid.*

* Strong breakout candle
* Volume increase (if available)
* Retest of breakout level

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid these:

* Forcing patterns where none exist
* Trading before breakout confirmation
* Ignoring trend direction
* Using patterns on very low timeframes only

---

## Pro Tip

Chart patterns are most powerful when combined with:

* Market structure
* Support & resistance
* Liquidity zones (Smart Money Concepts)

---

## Simple Trading Idea

* Reversal pattern at resistance → sell setup
* Reversal pattern at support → buy setup
* Continuation pattern → trade with trend
