# Swing Trading

Swing trading is a style where traders hold positions for **several days to weeks** to capture larger market moves.

It sits between:

* Day trading (short-term)
* Position trading (long-term)

---

## What is Swing Trading?

Swing trading focuses on:

> Capturing the “swings” in price within a larger trend.

Instead of reacting to small intraday movements, swing traders focus on bigger market structure shifts.

---

## Timeframes Used

Swing traders typically use:

* H4 (4-hour)
* Daily (D1)
* Weekly (W1) for direction

---

## How Swing Trading Works

A swing trader:

1. Identifies overall market trend
2. Waits for pullback or retracement
3. Enters at key zones (order blocks, FVG, support/resistance)
4. Holds trade for multiple days
5. Exits at major targets

---

## Why Swing Trading is Popular

Swing trading is preferred because:

* Less screen time
* Lower stress
* Bigger profit potential per trade
* More structured decisions

---

## Advantages of Swing Trading

* No need to monitor charts constantly
* Fewer emotional decisions
* Captures large market moves
* Works well with full-time jobs

---

## Disadvantages of Swing Trading

* Requires patience
* Trades take longer to play out
* Exposure to overnight news/events
* Requires strong discipline

---

## Core Swing Trading Strategy

A typical approach:

1. Identify higher timeframe trend
2. Mark key liquidity zones
3. Wait for pullback into order block or FVG
4. Confirm with structure (BOS/CHOCH)
5. Enter trade
6. Hold for large move

---

## Risk Management

Swing trading requires:

👉 Lower frequency, but well-planned risk

* Risk 1–2% per trade
* Wider stop losses
* Higher reward targets (1:3 or more)

---

## Best Market Conditions

Swing trading works best when:

* Market is trending
* Clear structure is visible
* Strong macro direction exists

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Entering too early without confirmation
* Ignoring higher timeframe trend
* Moving stop loss emotionally
* Closing trades too early due to fear

---

## Swing Trading Psychology

You must develop:

* Patience (holding trades for days)
* Confidence in analysis
* Emotional detachment from short-term noise

---

## Pro Tip

Swing trading is not about frequency.

It is about:

> Quality setups with strong confluence

---

## Simple Example Setup

* Daily trend is bullish
* Price retraces to bullish order block
* Liquidity is swept below
* CHOCH confirms reversal
* Entry taken with target at previous highs

---

## Reality Check

Swing trading is:

* Slower but more stable
* Less stressful than scalping/day trading
* Requires patience more than speed
