# Position Trading

Position trading is a long-term trading style where traders hold positions for **weeks, months, or even longer**.

It focuses on capturing **major market trends**, not small price fluctuations.

---

## What is Position Trading?

Position trading is based on:

> Identifying a strong macro trend and holding it until the trend weakens or reverses.

Unlike scalping or day trading, position traders ignore short-term noise.

---

## Timeframes Used

Position traders typically use:

* Daily (D1)
* Weekly (W1)
* Monthly (MN)

Lower timeframes are rarely used except for entry refinement.

---

## How Position Trading Works

A position trader:

1. Analyzes macro trend direction
2. Studies economic or fundamental context
3. Identifies long-term structure
4. Enters on retracements or breakouts
5. Holds position for extended periods
6. Exits when trend weakens

---

## Why Position Trading Works

Markets often move in:

* Long-term trends
* Multi-month expansions
* Macro cycles

👉 Position trading aims to capture these large movements.

---

## Advantages of Position Trading

* Very low stress
* Minimal screen time
* Large profit potential per trade
* Less emotional pressure

---

## Disadvantages of Position Trading

* Requires patience
* Capital is locked for long periods
* Requires strong conviction
* Exposure to macro news/events

---

## Core Position Trading Strategy

A typical approach:

1. Identify long-term trend (weekly/monthly)
2. Wait for major pullback
3. Enter at institutional zones (order blocks / FVG)
4. Hold through fluctuations
5. Exit at major structure levels

---

## Risk Management

Position trading requires:

* Lower frequency trading
* Strong capital protection
* Wider stop losses
* Higher reward expectations (1:5 or more)

---

## Market Conditions for Position Trading

Best when:

* Strong macro trend exists
* Central bank policy supports direction
* Market shows long-term structure

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Reacting to short-term volatility
* Exiting too early due to fear
* Ignoring macro trend direction
* Overleveraging long-term positions

---

## Position Trading Psychology

You need:

* High patience
* Strong conviction
* Emotional detachment
* Trust in analysis over time

---

## Pro Tip

Position trading is not about timing perfection.

It is about:

> Catching the major move and staying with it

---

## Simple Example Setup

* Weekly trend is bullish
* Price retraces into major order block
* Liquidity is swept on higher timeframe
* Entry taken on confirmation
* Trade held for weeks/months

---

## Reality Check

Position trading is:

* Slow but powerful
* Highly dependent on patience
* Best suited for long-term thinkers
