# Support & Resistance

If you ask any trader where they started…

Most will say: **Support and Resistance**.

These are the most basic—but powerful—tools in trading.

---

## What is Support?

Support is a price level where the market tends to **stop falling and bounce upward**.

👉 Why?

Because buyers step in at that level.

Think of support as a **floor** that price struggles to break below.

![Support and resistance levels](/support-resistance-levels.svg)

*Visualizing reaction zones helps you see where buyers and sellers are likely to step in.*

---

### Example:

* Price drops to a level multiple times
* Each time, it bounces up

👉 That level becomes support

---

## What is Resistance?

Resistance is a price level where the market tends to **stop rising and reverse downward**.

👉 Why?

Because sellers step in at that level.

Think of resistance as a **ceiling** that price struggles to break above.

---

### Example:

* Price rises to a level multiple times
* Each time, it drops

👉 That level becomes resistance

---

## Why Support & Resistance Work

Markets move based on psychology.

At key levels:

* Buyers believe price is cheap → they buy
* Sellers believe price is expensive → they sell

This creates repeated reactions at the same levels.

---

## Key Types of Levels

### 1. Strong Levels

* Multiple touches
* Clear rejections
* Visible on higher timeframes

👉 These are more reliable

---

### 2. Weak Levels

* Few touches
* Messy price action

👉 Less reliable

---

## The More Touches, The Better?

Not always.

* 2–3 clean touches = strong level
* Too many touches = level becomes weak

👉 Because liquidity gets consumed over time

---

## Breakouts

Sometimes price doesn’t respect a level—it breaks it.

This is called a **breakout**.

![Breakout example](/support-resistance-breakout.svg)

*When price clears a level with momentum, it may continue in that direction.*

### Breakout Example:

* Price breaks above resistance
  👉 Possible continuation upward

* Price breaks below support
  👉 Possible continuation downward

---

## Fake Breakouts (Very Important)

Not all breakouts are real.

Sometimes price:

* Breaks a level
* Traps traders
* Reverses quickly

👉 This is called a **false breakout** or **liquidity grab**

![False breakout warning](/support-resistance-fake-breakout.svg)

*Always confirm whether the move is genuine or just a trap before acting on it.*

---

## Support Becomes Resistance (Flip)

One of the most powerful concepts:

👉 When support breaks → it becomes resistance
👉 When resistance breaks → it becomes support

This is called a **level flip**.

---

### Example:

* Price breaks above resistance
* Comes back down
* Respects that same level

👉 That resistance is now support

---

## How to Draw Support & Resistance

Follow these simple rules:

1. Use higher timeframes first (H1, H4, Daily)
2. Mark clear highs and lows
3. Focus on zones, not exact lines
4. Avoid cluttering your chart

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid these:

* Drawing too many lines
* Treating levels as exact prices
* Ignoring trend direction
* Trading every touch blindly

---

## Pro Tip

Support & Resistance works best when combined with:

* Market structure
* Trend direction
* Confirmation (candles, patterns, etc.)

---

## Simple Strategy Idea

* In an uptrend → Buy at support
* In a downtrend → Sell at resistance

This is the foundation of many strategies.
