# Break of Structure (BOS)

Break of Structure is one of the most important confirmations in trading.

It tells you:

> The market is continuing in its current direction.

---

## What is Break of Structure?

A Break of Structure (BOS) happens when price:

👉 Breaks a previous significant high or low
👉 In the direction of the trend

---

## BOS in an Uptrend (Bullish BOS)

In an uptrend, price forms:

* Higher Highs (HH)
* Higher Lows (HL)

A bullish BOS occurs when:

👉 Price breaks above the previous high

This confirms:

![Bullish BOS visual](/bos-bullish.svg)

*A clean break above the previous high is a strong confirmation that the uptrend is still active.*

* Buyers are still strong
* Trend is continuing upward

---

## BOS in a Downtrend (Bearish BOS)

In a downtrend, price forms:

* Lower Highs (LH)
* Lower Lows (LL)

A bearish BOS occurs when:

👉 Price breaks below the previous low

This confirms:

![Bearish BOS visual](/bos-bearish.svg)

*A break below the previous low confirms that bearish momentum is still in control.*

* Sellers are still in control
* Trend is continuing downward

---

## Why BOS Matters

Without confirmation, trading becomes guessing.

BOS gives you:

* Confidence in direction
* Validation of trend
* Clear continuation signals

---

## BOS vs Fake Breakouts

Not every break is a real BOS.

A valid BOS usually has:

* Strong momentum (impulsive move)
* Clean break (not just a wick)
* Follow-through in the same direction

---

## BOS + Liquidity

One powerful concept:

👉 Liquidity is often taken BEFORE a BOS

Example:

![BOS after liquidity sweep](/bos-liquidity.svg)

*Liquidity is often taken first, and the real directional move follows with a structure break.*

1. Price sweeps equal lows (liquidity)
2. Then moves upward
3. Breaks previous high

👉 That break is a strong bullish BOS

---

## BOS + Order Blocks

High-probability setup:

* Price reacts from order block
* Then creates BOS

👉 Confirms institutional activity

---

## Entry Strategy Using BOS

Instead of entering randomly:

1. Wait for BOS
2. Wait for pullback (order block / FVG)
3. Enter in direction of BOS

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Treating every small break as BOS
* Ignoring higher timeframe structure
* Entering before confirmation
* Confusing BOS with reversal

---

## BOS vs Trend Reversal

Important distinction:

* BOS = continuation
* Reversal requires deeper confirmation

👉 That confirmation is called:

**Change of Character (CHOCH)**

---

## Pro Tip

Always ask:

👉 “Did the market break structure with strength?”

If not, don’t trust the move.

---

## Simple Trading Idea

* Uptrend + bullish BOS + pullback
  👉 Buy setup

* Downtrend + bearish BOS + pullback
  👉 Sell setup

---

## Reality Check

BOS is not a signal by itself.

It is:

* A confirmation tool
* A directional filter
* A piece of the puzzle
