# Trade Review

Trade review is the process of analyzing your past trades to improve future performance.

It answers one simple question:

> Did I follow my rules—and what can I improve?

---

## Why Trade Review is Important

Without review:

* You repeat mistakes
* You don’t learn from losses
* Your growth stays random

With review:

👉 Every trade becomes a lesson

---

## What to Review After Every Trade

---

### 1. Setup Quality

* Was the setup valid?
* Did it match your strategy rules?

---

### 2. Execution

* Did you enter too early or too late?
* Did you follow confirmation rules?

---

### 3. Risk Management

* Was risk properly calculated?
* Did you follow your 1–2% rule?

---

### 4. Emotional State

* Were you calm or emotional?
* Did fear or greed affect decisions?

---

### 5. Outcome (But Not the Focus)

* Win or loss is not the main focus
* Process is more important than result

---

## Winning Trade Review

Even winning trades should be reviewed:

* Did you follow rules?
* Was it luck or skill?
* Can it be repeated?

👉 A bad habit that wins is still a bad habit

---

## Losing Trade Review

Ask:

* Was the setup valid?
* Did I break any rules?
* Was it a good loss or bad loss?

👉 Good losses follow rules
👉 Bad losses break rules

---

## Common Mistakes in Trade Review

Avoid:

* Only reviewing losing trades
* Ignoring emotional mistakes
* Skipping documentation
* Blaming the market

---

## How Professionals Review Trades

Professionals:

* Record every trade
* Analyze patterns in mistakes
* Focus on process improvement
* Adjust behavior, not just strategy

---

## Simple Trade Review Format

After each trade, write:

* Setup type
* Entry reason
* Result
* Mistakes made
* Lesson learned

---

## Pro Tip

You don’t improve by trading more.

You improve by:

> Reviewing better

---

## Golden Rule

> A trader who reviews consistently will eventually outperform a trader who only executes.
