# Scalping

Scalping is a trading style where traders aim to take **small, quick profits from short-term price movements**.

Trades usually last:

> Seconds to minutes (sometimes up to a few hours)

---

## What is Scalping?

Scalping involves:

* Entering the market frequently
* Taking small profits
* Exiting trades quickly
* Avoiding long exposure

👉 The goal is not big moves—it’s small consistent gains.

---

## Why Traders Use Scalping

Scalping is used because:

* It offers quick opportunities
* Reduces exposure to market risk
* Works in volatile conditions
* Allows multiple trades per day

---

## Timeframes Used

Scalpers typically use:

* M1 (1-minute)
* M5 (5-minute)
* M15 (for confirmation)

---

## How Scalping Works

A scalper looks for:

* Small price imbalances
* Quick momentum shifts
* Liquidity grabs
* Fast reversals

👉 Enter → capture small move → exit quickly

---

## Advantages of Scalping

* Fast results
* Frequent opportunities
* No overnight risk
* Good in volatile markets

---

## Disadvantages of Scalping

* High stress
* Requires focus and speed
* High transaction costs (spread/fees)
* Emotionally demanding

---

## Key Skills Needed

To scalp successfully, you need:

* Fast decision-making
* Strong discipline
* Emotional control
* Precise entries
* Strict risk management

---

## Scalping Strategy Basics

A simple scalping approach:

1. Identify short-term trend
2. Wait for pullback or liquidity sweep
3. Enter quickly on confirmation
4. Exit at small target (1:1 or 1:2 RR)

---

## Risk Management in Scalping

Even in scalping:

👉 Never risk more than 1% per trade

Because:

* Losses can accumulate quickly
* Many trades are taken per day

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Overtrading out of excitement
* Ignoring spreads and fees
* Entering without confirmation
* Moving stop loss impulsively

---

## Scalping Psychology

Scalping requires:

* Calm under pressure
* No emotional attachment to trades
* Ability to accept small losses quickly

---

## Pro Tip

Scalping is not about speed alone.

It is about:

> Precision + discipline + consistency

---

## When NOT to Scalp

Avoid scalping when:

* Market is slow or ranging unpredictably
* You are emotionally unstable
* You are tired or unfocused

---

## Reality Check

Scalping is:

* Not easy
* Not relaxing
* Not suitable for beginners without practice
