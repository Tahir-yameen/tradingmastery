# Change of Character (CHOCH)

If Break of Structure (BOS) confirms continuation…

Then Change of Character (CHOCH) signals:

> A potential shift in market direction.

---

## What is CHOCH?

CHOCH happens when the market:

👉 Breaks structure in the opposite direction of the current trend

This is often the **first sign of a possible reversal**.

---

## CHOCH in an Uptrend → Bearish Shift

In an uptrend, price forms:

* Higher Highs (HH)
* Higher Lows (HL)

A CHOCH occurs when:

👉 Price breaks below the previous Higher Low (HL)

This signals:

![CHOCH in uptrend](/choch-uptrend.svg)

*A break below the previous higher low often marks the beginning of a bearish shift.*

* Buyers are weakening
* Sellers are entering
* Trend may reverse downward

---

## CHOCH in a Downtrend → Bullish Shift

In a downtrend, price forms:

* Lower Highs (LH)
* Lower Lows (LL)

A CHOCH occurs when:

👉 Price breaks above the previous Lower High (LH)

This signals:

![CHOCH in downtrend](/choch-downtrend.svg)

*A break above the previous lower high often marks the start of a bullish reversal.*

* Sellers are weakening
* Buyers are stepping in
* Trend may reverse upward

---

## CHOCH vs BOS (Important Difference)

Many beginners confuse these.

👉 BOS = continuation
👉 CHOCH = potential reversal

---

### Example:

* Uptrend → price breaks previous high
  👉 BOS (trend continues)

* Uptrend → price breaks previous low
  👉 CHOCH (possible reversal)

---

## Why CHOCH Matters

CHOCH gives you:

* Early warning of trend change
* Opportunity to enter new trend early
* Better risk-to-reward setups

---

## CHOCH + Liquidity (High Probability Setup)

This is where it becomes powerful:

1. Price sweeps liquidity (above highs or below lows)
2. Then breaks structure in opposite direction (CHOCH)

![CHOCH + liquidity sweep](/choch-liquidity.svg)

*This combination is often a strong early warning of a real trend shift.*

👉 This is a strong reversal signal

---

## CHOCH + Order Block + FVG

Advanced confluence:

* Liquidity sweep
* CHOCH confirmation
* Price returns to order block / FVG

👉 High probability entry zone

---

## Entry Strategy Using CHOCH

1. Identify existing trend
2. Wait for liquidity sweep
3. Confirm CHOCH
4. Wait for pullback
5. Enter in new direction

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Confusing CHOCH with small pullbacks
* Ignoring higher timeframe trend
* Entering immediately after CHOCH without confirmation
* Trading every CHOCH blindly

---

## Pro Tip

CHOCH alone is not enough.

Always confirm with:

* Liquidity
* Structure
* Entry model (OB or FVG)

---

## Simple Trading Idea

* Uptrend + liquidity sweep + CHOCH
  👉 Look for sell setup

* Downtrend + liquidity sweep + CHOCH
  👉 Look for buy setup

---

## Reality Check

CHOCH is:

* An early signal—not a guarantee
* A warning—not a confirmation alone

It must be combined with other concepts.
