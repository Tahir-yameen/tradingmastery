# Emotions in Trading

Trading is not just a technical game.

It is a psychological battle between:

> Logic vs Emotion

And in most cases…

👉 Emotion wins for beginners.

---

## The Two Main Emotions

There are two emotions that control most trading mistakes:

* Fear
* Greed

---

## 1. Fear

Fear appears when:

* You are afraid to lose money
* You hesitate to enter trades
* You exit trades too early
* You avoid valid setups

### Effects of Fear:

* Missed opportunities
* Small profits instead of big moves
* Lack of confidence

---

## 2. Greed

Greed appears when:

* You want more profit than planned
* You overtrade
* You ignore stop loss
* You move take profit further

### Effects of Greed:

* Big losses
* Account drawdown
* Broken trading rules

---

## Fear vs Greed Cycle

Trading often becomes a cycle:

1. Fear after loss
2. Missed trade
3. Greed to recover
4. Overtrading
5. Bigger loss
6. More fear

👉 This cycle destroys accounts

---

## FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)

FOMO happens when:

* Price is already moving
* You enter late
* Without confirmation

👉 You chase the market instead of waiting

---

## Revenge Trading

After a loss, traders often:

* Double their lot size
* Enter random trades
* Try to recover quickly

👉 This is one of the fastest ways to blow an account

---

## How Professionals Handle Emotions

Professional traders:

* Accept losses calmly
* Follow rules strictly
* Do not chase trades
* Stay emotionally neutral

---

## Emotional Control Techniques

---

### 1. Predefined Rules

If rules are clear:

👉 emotions have less control

---

### 2. Risk Limitation

Small risk per trade reduces emotional pressure

---

### 3. Break After Losses

Step away after losing streaks

---

### 4. No Impulsive Trading

If setup is not clear:

👉 do not trade

---

## The Truth About Emotions

You cannot remove emotions.

But you can:

> Train yourself to not react to them.

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Entering trades out of excitement
* Closing winners too early
* Holding losers too long
* Trading based on stress

---

## Pro Tip

Your biggest enemy is not the market.

It is:

> Your reaction to the market

---

## Golden Rule

> If emotions control your trading, you are not trading—you are gambling.
