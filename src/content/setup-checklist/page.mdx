# Setup Checklist

A setup checklist is a simple but powerful tool that ensures:

> You only enter trades that meet your rules.

It removes emotional decision-making and replaces it with structure.

---

## Why a Checklist is Important

Without a checklist:

* You enter impulsively
* You ignore rules
* You overtrade
* You lose consistency

With a checklist:

👉 Every trade is intentional

---

## Pre-Trade Checklist (Core Rules)

Before entering any trade, ask:

---

### 1. Market Structure

* Is the market trending or ranging?
* Am I trading with the structure?

---

### 2. Liquidity Check

* Has liquidity been taken?
* Are there obvious highs or lows targeted?

---

### 3. Confirmation

* Is there BOS or CHOCH?
* Is direction confirmed?

---

### 4. Entry Zone

* Is price at an order block or FVG?
* Is the entry area valid?

---

### 5. Risk-Reward

* Is RR at least 1:2 or better?
* Is stop loss logical and safe?

---

### 6. Risk Management

* Am I risking only 1–2%?
* Am I emotionally stable right now?

---

## If ANY Answer is “No”

👉 You do NOT take the trade

---

## Why This Works

A checklist helps you:

* Avoid emotional entries
* Stay consistent
* Filter low-quality setups
* Build discipline over time

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Skipping checklist “just this once”
* Forcing trades to match rules
* Ignoring confirmation steps
* Trading without structure

---

## Pro Tip

The best traders are not those who find the most setups.

They are those who:

> Filter the most bad trades

---

## Psychological Benefit

A checklist helps reduce:

* Anxiety
* Overthinking
* FOMO
* Emotional pressure

---

## Simple Rule

> If it doesn’t pass the checklist, it doesn’t deserve capital.
