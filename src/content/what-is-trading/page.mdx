# What is Trading?

At its core, trading is very simple.

It is the act of **buying and selling an asset** with the goal of making a profit from price movement.

But while the idea is simple… the execution is where most people struggle.

---

## The Basic Idea of Trading

Every market works on one simple principle:

> Prices move because of **supply and demand**

* When more people want to **buy**, price goes up
* When more people want to **sell**, price goes down

As a trader, your job is to **predict these movements** and position yourself accordingly.

![Supply and demand in trading](/trading-idea.svg)

*Visualizing how price reacts to buyers and sellers helps make the core idea of trading easier to understand.*

---

## A Simple Real-Life Example

Imagine you buy a mobile phone for $500.

After some time, demand increases and you sell it for $600.

👉 Your profit = $100

That’s trading.

Now imagine the opposite:

You buy at $500, but the price drops to $450.

👉 Your loss = $50

This is also part of trading—and it's completely normal.

---

## Types of Trading Markets

There are different financial markets where trading happens. Each has its own characteristics.

![Trading markets overview](/trading-markets.svg)

*Different markets offer different styles, liquidity, and volatility levels.*

### 1. Forex (Foreign Exchange Market)

* Trading currencies (e.g., EUR/USD, GBP/USD)
* Largest market in the world
* Open 24 hours (5 days a week)
* Highly liquid

---

### 2. Stock Market

* Buying shares of companies (e.g., Apple, Tesla)
* Influenced by company performance and news
* Usually slower compared to Forex or crypto

---

### 3. Cryptocurrency Market

* Digital currencies (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum)
* Very volatile (prices move fast)
* Open 24/7

---

### 4. Commodities Market

* Physical assets like gold, oil, silver
* Often influenced by global events and economic conditions

---

## Types of Trades

There are only two types of trades you can take:

### 1. Buy (Long)

You buy when you believe the price will go **up**.

👉 Buy low → Sell high → Profit

---

### 2. Sell (Short)

You sell when you believe the price will go **down**.

👉 Sell high → Buy low → Profit

This is something unique in trading—you can make money even when markets fall.

---

## How Trading Actually Works (Behind the Scenes)

When you place a trade:

* You are entering a position in the market
* Your broker connects you to that market
* Your profit or loss depends on price movement

Example:

* You buy EUR/USD at 1.1000
* Price moves to 1.1050
  👉 You make profit

If it drops instead:
👉 You take a loss

## Important Terms You Should Know

Before moving forward, understand these basic terms:

* **Asset** → What you are trading (currency, stock, crypto)
* **Price** → Current value of the asset
* **Spread** → Difference between buy and sell price
* **Lot size** → Trade size
* **Leverage** → Borrowed capital to increase position size

Don't worry—we’ll cover these in detail later.

## Trading vs Investing

Many beginners confuse these two.

### Trading:

* Short-term
* Frequent buying and selling
* Focus on price movements

### Investing:

* Long-term
* Holding assets for months or years
* Focus on value growth

Both are valid—but this book focuses on **trading**.

---

## The Truth About Profits

Let’s clear a big misconception:

👉 Trading is NOT about winning every trade.

Even professional traders:

* Lose trades regularly
* Focus on managing losses
* Aim for consistency, not perfection

Your goal is:

> Win more than you lose—and control your risk

![Risk management visual](/trading-risk.svg)

*Good traders protect capital first, then focus on growth.*

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid these early on:

* Trading without knowledge
* Risking too much on one trade
* Following random signals
* Overtrading
* Letting emotions control decisions
