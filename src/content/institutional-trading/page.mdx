# Institutional Trading

Institutional trading refers to the way large financial players operate in the market, such as:

* Banks
* Hedge funds
* Investment firms
* Liquidity providers

These participants control **the majority of market volume**.

---

## Why Institutions Matter

Retail traders often think they control price.

In reality:

> Institutions move the market, retail follows it.

Because institutions trade:

* Massive capital
* Large order sizes
* Long-term positions

They cannot operate like retail traders.

---

## The Problem Institutions Face

If a large institution wants to buy or sell:

👉 They cannot enter all at once

Because:

* It would move the price too quickly
* It would increase their entry cost
* It would expose their intent

So they must:

> Build positions gradually and strategically

---

## How Institutions Enter the Market

They use a process called:

### 1. Accumulation

* Quietly building buy positions
* Often during sideways or slow markets

### 2. Manipulation

* Creating false moves
* Triggering retail stop losses
* Grabbing liquidity

### 3. Distribution

* Moving price strongly in intended direction
* Taking profit from large moves

---

## Liquidity is the Key

Institutions need liquidity to trade.

They look for:

* Stop losses above highs
* Stop losses below lows
* Retail breakout entries

👉 These areas provide the volume they need

---

## Why Price Moves in Waves

Markets move in cycles because institutions:

* Accumulate positions
* Push price to grab liquidity
* Then continue trend

This creates:

* Expansion
* Retracement
* Continuation

---

## Institutional Footprints

On charts, institutions leave clues:

* Liquidity sweeps
* Strong impulsive moves
* Order blocks
* Fair value gaps

👉 These are not random—they are structured behavior

---

## Why Retail Traders Lose

Retail traders often:

* Enter too early
* Trade breakouts blindly
* Ignore liquidity zones
* Use emotional decisions

Meanwhile institutions:

* Take liquidity first
* Enter at better prices
* Move price in their favor

---

## Institutional Trading Mindset

Institutions think in terms of:

* Liquidity
* Efficiency
* Execution cost
* Position building

Not:

* Indicators
* Signals
* Small timeframe noise

---

## How You Should Think Now

Instead of asking:

❌ “Where should I enter?”

Start asking:

✅ “Where are institutions likely to enter or manipulate price?”
