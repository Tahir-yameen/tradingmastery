# Day Trading

Day trading is a trading style where positions are opened and closed **within the same trading day**.

No trades are held overnight.

---

## What is Day Trading?

Day trading focuses on:

* Capturing intraday price movements
* Avoiding overnight risk
* Trading active market sessions
* Using structured setups

---

## Timeframes Used

Day traders commonly use:

* M5 (5-minute)
* M15 (15-minute)
* H1 (1-hour for direction)

---

## How Day Trading Works

A typical day trader:

1. Analyzes market structure
2. Identifies key levels (support/resistance, liquidity)
3. Waits for setup during active sessions
4. Enters trade with confirmation
5. Exits before the day ends

---

## Best Trading Sessions

Day trading is most effective during:

### 1. London Session

* High volatility
* Strong directional moves
* Best for setups

### 2. New York Session

* High liquidity
* News-driven moves
* Continuation or reversals

---

## Advantages of Day Trading

* No overnight risk
* Multiple opportunities daily
* Balanced pace (not too fast, not too slow)
* Good for beginners and professionals

---

## Disadvantages of Day Trading

* Requires screen time
* Emotional discipline needed
* Can lead to overtrading
* Requires consistent focus

---

## Core Day Trading Strategy Flow

A simple structure:

1. Identify trend (market structure)
2. Mark liquidity zones
3. Wait for liquidity sweep
4. Confirm CHOCH or BOS
5. Enter on retracement (OB or FVG)
6. Take profit intraday

---

## Risk Management

Even in day trading:

👉 Risk 1–2% per trade
👉 Avoid multiple high-risk trades per day

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Trading outside sessions
* Overtrading every small move
* Ignoring higher timeframe direction
* Entering without confirmation

---

## Day Trading Psychology

You must be:

* Patient (wait for setup)
* Disciplined (follow rules)
* Emotionally stable (no revenge trading)

---

## Pro Tip

Day trading is not about constant action.

It is about:

> Waiting for high-quality intraday setups

---

## Simple Example Setup

* London session begins
* Price sweeps liquidity
* CHOCH confirms reversal
* Price returns to order block
* Entry taken with 1:2 RR

---

## Reality Check

Day trading is:

* Structured but demanding
* Profitable when disciplined
* Difficult when emotional
