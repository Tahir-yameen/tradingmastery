# Market Participants

The market does not move randomly.

Every price movement you see on a chart is caused by **people and institutions placing orders**.

To become a successful trader, you must understand:

> Who is in the market—and what they want.

![Market participants overview](/participants-market-flow.svg)

*Retail traders, institutions, and price movement all connect through liquidity and order flow.*

---

## The Two Main Types of Traders

There are two major groups in the market:

1. Retail Traders (Individuals)
2. Institutional Traders (Smart Money)

---

## 1. Retail Traders (The Majority)

Retail traders are people like:

* You
* Beginners
* Small account holders
* Independent traders working from home

They typically:

* Trade small amounts of money
* Use basic strategies (support/resistance, indicators)
* Follow signals or social media
* React emotionally

👉 Important: Retail traders do NOT move the market.

---

## 2. Institutional Traders (Smart Money)

These are the real movers of the market.

They include:

* Banks
* Hedge funds
* Investment firms
* Large financial institutions

They trade with:

* Millions or billions of dollars
* Advanced strategies
* Deep market knowledge

👉 When institutions enter the market, **price moves**.

---

## Why This Matters

Most beginners trade like this:

* Buy at obvious support
* Sell at obvious resistance
* Place stop losses in predictable places

Institutions know this.

👉 And they use it to their advantage.

![Liquidity concept](/participants-liquidity.svg)

*Liquidity pools are where large orders are absorbed and where smart money often operates.*

---

## The Concept of Liquidity

Liquidity is where money exists in the market.

It is often found:

* Above highs (buy stops)
* Below lows (sell stops)

Institutions need liquidity to:

* Enter large positions
* Exit large positions

So what do they do?

👉 They push price into these areas.

---

## Stop Hunting (Reality Check)

You may have experienced this:

* You enter a trade
* Price hits your stop loss
* Then moves exactly in your direction

This is not random.

👉 This is often called a **liquidity grab**.

Institutions:

* Trigger retail stop losses
* Collect liquidity
* Then move price in the real direction

![Liquidity grab example](/participants-stop-hunt.svg)

*This is why stop-loss zones can become magnets before a strong move begins.*

---

## Smart Money vs Retail Thinking

### Retail Mindset:

* “Price is at support → I will buy”
* “Price is at resistance → I will sell”

### Smart Money Mindset:

* “Where are retail traders placing stops?”
* “Where is liquidity?”
* “How can I use that to enter efficiently?”

---

## The Goal as a Trader

You don’t want to trade like the majority.

You want to:

> Think like institutions, not react like retail.

---

## Key Takeaways

* Markets are controlled by large institutions
* Retail traders follow—institutions lead
* Liquidity drives price movement
* Stop losses are often targets, not protection

---

## Important Mindset Shift

Stop asking:

❌ “Where should I buy or sell?”

Start asking:

✅ “Where is liquidity—and what is smart money likely to do?”
