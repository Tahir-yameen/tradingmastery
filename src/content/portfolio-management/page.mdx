# Portfolio Management

Portfolio management is the process of **organizing and balancing your trading capital across multiple positions, strategies, and risk levels**.

It is what transforms a trader into a long-term capital manager.

---

## What is a Trading Portfolio?

A trading portfolio is:

> The total collection of your trading capital and all active positions.

It is not just one trade—it is the entire system of risk and exposure.

---

## Why Portfolio Management Matters

Without portfolio management:

* One bad trade can damage your account
* Risk becomes unbalanced
* Emotional pressure increases
* Growth becomes inconsistent

With it:

👉 Risk is controlled across all trades

---

## Core Principles

---

### 1. Capital Preservation

The first goal is not profit:

> It is protecting your capital

If capital is safe, opportunities always remain.

---

### 2. Diversification

Do not rely on one:

* Asset
* Pair
* Strategy
* Timeframe

👉 Spread exposure intelligently

---

### 3. Risk Allocation

Instead of risking equally on every trade:

* High confidence setup → standard risk
* Medium setup → reduced risk
* Low confidence → no trade

---

### 4. Correlation Awareness

Many assets move together.

Example:

* EUR/USD and GBP/USD often correlate
* Crypto pairs often move in clusters

👉 Overexposure increases hidden risk

---

## Portfolio Risk Rule

A professional approach:

> Total portfolio risk should remain controlled (e.g., 3–6% max exposure at any time)

Even if multiple trades are open.

---

### 2. Multi-Strategy Portfolio

### 3. Multi-Asset Portfolio

## Equity Curve Thinking

Portfolio management focuses on:

> Smooth, stable growth—not explosive gains

Ideal equity curve:

* Steady upward movement
* Controlled drawdowns
* No extreme spikes or crashes

---

## Risk Management Across Portfolio

Key rules:

* Never overexpose capital in one direction
* Avoid stacking correlated trades
* Reduce risk during drawdowns
* Increase only during consistency phases

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Putting all capital into one trade
* Overleveraging multiple positions
* Ignoring correlation risk
* Chasing profits instead of managing exposure

---

## Pro Insight

Professionals do NOT think in terms of:

❌ “How much can I make on this trade?”

They think:

> “How does this trade affect my entire portfolio risk?”
