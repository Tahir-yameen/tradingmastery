# Indicators

Indicators are tools that help you analyze price movements using formulas based on past data.

They are designed to answer questions like:

* Is the market overbought or oversold?
* Is the trend strong or weak?
* Is momentum increasing or decreasing?

But here is the truth:

> Indicators do NOT predict the market. They only react to it.

---

## Why Indicators Exist

Price charts can be noisy and hard to interpret.

Indicators help by:

* Smoothing price data
* Highlighting momentum
* Identifying trends
* Finding possible reversals

---

## Types of Indicators

There are three main categories:

---

## 1. Trend Indicators

These help identify the direction of the market.

---

### 📊 Moving Averages (MA)

A moving average shows the average price over a specific period.

Common types:

* SMA (Simple Moving Average)
* EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

### What it tells you:

![Moving averages visual](/indicator-moving-average.svg)

*Moving averages smooth price movement and help show the dominant trend direction.*

* Price above MA → bullish trend
* Price below MA → bearish trend

---

### Golden Cross / Death Cross

* Golden Cross → short MA crosses above long MA (bullish)
* Death Cross → short MA crosses below long MA (bearish)

---

## 2. Momentum Indicators

These show how strong a move is.

---

### 📉 RSI (Relative Strength Index)

RSI measures speed and strength of price movement.

![RSI momentum visual](/indicator-rsi.svg)

*RSI helps you spot momentum strength and possible exhaustion zones.*

* Above 70 → overbought
* Below 30 → oversold

But important:

👉 Overbought does NOT mean sell immediately
👉 Oversold does NOT mean buy immediately

---

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

MACD shows momentum shifts using moving averages.

* Bullish crossover → possible upward move
* Bearish crossover → possible downward move

---

## 3. Volatility Indicators

These show how much the market is moving.

---

### 📦 Bollinger Bands

Bollinger Bands expand and contract based on volatility.

![Bollinger bands visual](/indicator-bollinger.svg)

*When the bands squeeze, volatility is low and a breakout may be forming.*

* Tight bands → low volatility (possible breakout coming)
* Wide bands → high volatility

---

## The Biggest Mistake with Indicators

Most beginners:

* Use too many indicators
* Wait for “perfect confirmation”
* Get conflicting signals

👉 This leads to confusion, not clarity.

---

## The Truth About Indicators

Indicators:

* Follow price
* Do NOT lead price
* Lag behind market movement

👉 Price action is always first.

---

## How Professionals Use Indicators

Professionals use indicators as:

* Confirmation tools
* Not decision-making tools

They rely more on:

* Market structure
* Liquidity
* Price action

---

## Simple Example Strategy

Instead of:

* “RSI says overbought, so I sell”

Use:

* “Market is in resistance zone + bearish structure + RSI is overbought → strong confluence”

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Using 5–10 indicators at once
* Trading only based on RSI or MACD
* Ignoring price structure
* Blindly trusting signals

---

## Pro Tip

Less is more.

A clean chart with:

* Price structure
* Support/resistance
* 1–2 indicators max

…is far more powerful than a cluttered chart.
