# Order Blocks

Order Blocks are areas where **large institutions place their orders**.

They represent the origin of strong price moves.

If liquidity tells you *where price is going…*
Order blocks help you understand:

> Where price is likely to react from.

---

## What is an Order Block?

An order block is:

👉 The **last opposing candle** before a strong move in the market.

---

### Types of Order Blocks

---

## 1. Bullish Order Block

* Found before a strong upward move
* Usually the last bearish candle before price rises

👉 Indicates buying interest from institutions

![Bullish order block](/order-block-bullish.svg)

*This is the zone where institutions often accumulate before a strong upward move.*

---

## 2. Bearish Order Block

* Found before a strong downward move
* Usually the last bullish candle before price drops

👉 Indicates selling interest from institutions

![Bearish order block](/order-block-bearish.svg)

*This is the zone where institutions often distribute before a strong downward move.*

---

## Why Order Blocks Work

Institutions cannot enter all positions at once.

They:

* Accumulate orders
* Move price
* Then return to fill remaining positions

👉 This creates zones where price often comes back.

---

## The Concept of Mitigation

When price returns to an order block, it is called:

👉 **Mitigation**

This is where:

![Mitigation / return to block](/order-block-mitigation.svg)

*When price comes back to an order block, it may react again as institutions continue filling.*

* Institutions complete their orders
* Price often reacts again

---

## How to Identify a Valid Order Block

Look for:

1. Strong impulsive move away
2. Clear break of structure after the move
3. The last opposite candle before that move

👉 That candle becomes your order block zone

---

## Order Block vs Supply & Demand

They are similar—but not identical.

* Supply/Demand = broader zones
* Order Blocks = more precise, institutional-based zones

---

## High Probability Order Blocks

The best ones have:

* Strong displacement (big move)
* Break of structure
* Confluence with liquidity zones
* Alignment with trend

---

## Entry Concept

Instead of chasing price:

👉 Wait for price to return to the order block

Then:

* Look for confirmation
* Enter with better risk-to-reward

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Marking every candle as an order block
* Ignoring market structure
* Entering without confirmation
* Using order blocks in ranging markets

---

## Pro Tip

Order blocks are strongest when:

* They cause a break of structure
* They align with higher timeframe direction
* They exist near liquidity zones

---

## Simple Trading Idea

* Uptrend + bullish order block + liquidity sweep
  👉 Buy setup

* Downtrend + bearish order block + liquidity sweep
  👉 Sell setup

---

## Reality Check

Order blocks are not magic zones.

They are:

* Areas of interest
* Not guaranteed reversal points

Always combine with:

* Structure
* Liquidity
* Confirmation
