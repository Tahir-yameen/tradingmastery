# Market Structure

If trading had a foundation… this would be it.

Market structure is simply the way **price moves over time**.
It tells you whether the market is going:

* Up
* Down
* Or nowhere

Understanding this allows you to stop guessing—and start reading the market logically.

![Price structure overview](/market-structure-overview.svg)

*Visual clue: price forms a clear pattern of swings, which helps you identify the market direction.*

---

## What is Market Structure?

Market structure is the pattern created by price as it forms:

* Highs
* Lows

By studying these highs and lows, you can determine the direction of the market.

---

## The Three Types of Market Structure

There are only three possible conditions in any market:

![Three market conditions](/market-structure-types.svg)

*Uptrend, downtrend, and sideways movement are the three structural states you should learn to recognize.*

---

### 1. Uptrend (Bullish Market)

An uptrend is when price is moving upward.

It forms a pattern of:

* Higher Highs (HH)
* Higher Lows (HL)

👉 This means buyers are in control.

**Example Logic:**

* Price makes a high
* Pulls back but doesn’t drop too far
* Then creates a new higher high

This shows strength.

---

### 2. Downtrend (Bearish Market)

A downtrend is when price is moving downward.

It forms:

* Lower Highs (LH)
* Lower Lows (LL)

👉 This means sellers are in control.

**Example Logic:**

* Price drops
* Pulls back slightly
* Then drops even lower

This shows weakness.

---

### 3. Sideways (Ranging Market)

This is when price moves within a range.

* No clear higher highs
* No clear lower lows
* Price bounces between support and resistance

👉 This means the market is indecisive.

---

## Why Market Structure Matters

Most beginners:

* Enter trades randomly
* Ignore the trend
* Trade against the market

Professionals do the opposite:

> They trade **with the structure**, not against it.

![Structure-based trading](/market-structure-trend.svg)

*Following structure helps you avoid random entries and stay aligned with the trend.*

---

## The Golden Rule of Trading

👉 **"The trend is your friend."**

* In an uptrend → Look for buy opportunities
* In a downtrend → Look for sell opportunities

This alone can dramatically improve your results.

---

## Break of Structure (BOS)

A **Break of Structure** happens when the market changes its pattern.

Example:

* Market was making higher highs and higher lows
* Suddenly, it breaks below the previous low

👉 This signals a possible shift from uptrend to downtrend

This is one of the earliest signs of a **trend reversal**.

---

## Change of Character (CHOCH)

This is a more subtle shift in market behavior.

It often happens before a full trend reversal.

👉 Think of it as a warning sign:
"The market is about to change direction."

We will cover this deeply in the Smart Money Concepts section.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid these:

* Trading against the trend
* Ignoring higher timeframes
* Misidentifying structure
* Entering trades in sideways markets

---

## Pro Tip

Always start your analysis from a higher timeframe (like 1H or 4H), then move to lower timeframes.

This gives you:

* Clear direction
* Better entries
* Less noise
