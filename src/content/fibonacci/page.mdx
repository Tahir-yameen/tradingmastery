# Fibonacci Retracement

Fibonacci retracement is a tool used to identify **possible pullback zones** in a trend.

It is based on the idea that markets rarely move in a straight line—they move in waves.

---

## What is Fibonacci Retracement?

It is a set of horizontal levels drawn between:

* A swing low → Swing high (in an uptrend)
* A swing high → Swing low (in a downtrend)

These levels show where price might temporarily reverse before continuing the trend.

![Fibonacci retracement zones](/fibonacci-retracement.svg)

*Retracement levels highlight where price may pause or reverse during a trend.*

---

## Key Fibonacci Levels

The most commonly used levels are:

* 0.236
* 0.382
* 0.500
* 0.618 (most important)
* 0.786

👉 These are called “retracement levels”

---

## Why Fibonacci Works

Fibonacci works because of:

* Market psychology
* Natural wave-like price movement
* Trader behavior clustering around similar levels

It is not magic—it is **self-fulfilling behavior**.

---

## How to Use Fibonacci in an Uptrend

1. Identify a strong upward move
2. Draw Fibonacci from swing low → swing high
3. Wait for price to retrace back

### Key idea:

👉 You don’t chase price—you wait for pullback

---

## Best Buy Zone in Uptrend

The most important zone is:

👉 0.5 – 0.618 area (Golden Zone)

This is where:

![Golden zone visual](/fibonacci-golden-zone.svg)

*The 0.618 area is one of the most watched retracement zones in trading.*

* Buyers often re-enter
* Institutions may accumulate positions
* Price often resumes upward trend

---

## How to Use Fibonacci in a Downtrend

1. Identify a strong downward move
2. Draw Fibonacci from swing high → swing low
3. Wait for retracement upward

### Key idea:

👉 Sell during pullback, not after breakout

---

## The Golden Zone

The most important concept:

> 0.618 retracement level

This is often called the **golden ratio level**.

Why it matters:

* Strong reaction point
* High probability reversal zone
* Frequently respected in trends

---

## Fibonacci + Other Tools

Fibonacci works best when combined with:

![Confluence visual](/fibonacci-confluence.svg)

*Fibonacci becomes more powerful when it lines up with structure, support, and trendlines.*

* Support & resistance
* Market structure
* Trendlines
* Liquidity zones

👉 Alone = weak
👉 With confluence = powerful

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Drawing Fibonacci randomly
* Using it in sideways markets
* Entering trades only because price touches a level
* Ignoring trend direction

---

## Pro Tip

Always use Fibonacci in:

👉 Strong trending markets only

If there is no trend, Fibonacci loses meaning.

---

## Simple Trading Idea

* Uptrend + retracement to 0.618 + support zone
  👉 Buy setup

* Downtrend + retracement to 0.618 + resistance zone
  👉 Sell setup

---

## Reality Check

Fibonacci is not a prediction tool.

It is:

* A probability zone tool
* A planning tool
* A confluence tool
