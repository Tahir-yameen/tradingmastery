# Performance Analysis

Performance analysis is the process of evaluating your **overall trading results over time**.

Instead of focusing on one trade, you study:

> patterns across many trades

---

## Why Performance Analysis Matters

Without performance tracking:

* You don’t know if your strategy works
* You repeat hidden mistakes
* You rely on feelings instead of data

With it:

👉 You trade based on facts, not emotions

---

## Key Metrics to Track

---

### 1. Win Rate

> Percentage of winning trades

Example:

* 50 trades
* 25 wins

👉 Win rate = 50%

---

### 2. Risk-Reward Ratio

Average reward compared to risk:

* 1:2
* 1:3
* etc.

👉 This determines long-term profitability

---

### 3. Profit Factor

> Total profit ÷ total loss

* Above 1 = profitable
* Below 1 = losing strategy

---

### 4. Drawdown

Maximum loss period:

* Shows risk exposure
* Helps measure emotional pressure

---

### 5. Consistency

Are your results:

* Stable
* Random
* Improving over time

👉 Consistency matters more than big wins

---

## Why Win Rate Alone is Misleading

Example:

* 80% win rate
* 1:0.5 RR

👉 Still unprofitable long-term

Now compare:

* 40% win rate
* 1:3 RR

👉 Profitable over time

---

## Equity Curve

Your equity curve shows:

* Account growth
* Drawdowns
* Stability over time

Ideal curve:

> Smooth upward progression with small drawdowns

---

## Monthly Review

At the end of each month:

Ask:

* Did I follow my plan?
* Did my strategy perform as expected?
* Did emotions affect results?

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

* Only focusing on profit amount
* Ignoring losing streak patterns
* Not recording trades properly
* Judging performance after few trades

---

## Pro Tip

Your goal is not to make one big winning trade.

Your goal is:

> Repeatable, measurable performance over time

---

## Data vs Emotion

Performance analysis replaces:

❌ “I feel like I’m improving”

with:

✅ “My data shows I’m improving”

---

## Golden Rule

> What gets measured gets improved.
